Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, we offer a 30-day return policy with full refunds on eligible items.

How Returns Work

1. Initiate a Return

Contact us at returns@maisonfurniture.com within 30 days of receiving your order. Include your order number and the reason for the return. We will send you a return authorization and prepaid shipping label.

2. Pack & Ship

Securely pack the item in its original packaging if possible. Attach the prepaid shipping label to the package and drop it off at your nearest carrier location. Keep your tracking number for reference.

3. Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will process your refund within 5-7 business days. The refund will be credited to your original payment method. You will receive an email confirmation.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be in their original condition, unused and unwashed
  • All tags, labels, and original packaging must be included
  • Custom or personalized items are non-refundable
  • Sale and clearance items are final sale unless defective
  • Gift cards are non-refundable and non-transferable

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund immediately, no return required for severely damaged items.
